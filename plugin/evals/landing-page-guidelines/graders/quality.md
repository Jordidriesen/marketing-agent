PASS if all of these hold. (1) The reply names the page type as a landing page and names the structural framework used (StoryBrand or PAS). (2) It says that no brand kit exists and a generic voice was used. (3) The copy has a hero, exactly three benefit blocks and one primary call to action. (4) Every product claim traces to the four facts in the brief; there are no invented statistics, customers, compliance claims, integrations or service promises, and missing proof appears as a placeholder. (5) The call to action names the demo as the brief describes it (20 minutes, free) and does not add other promises. (6) The language is plain, specific and active, without unsupported buzzwords such as streamline, innovative or seamless. (7) It flags visual or layout cues for design. (8) The page copy, excluding notes, is roughly within 350 words. The reply must not stall on questions, because the brief is complete. FAIL otherwise.
