# Customer Story Brief Template

Use this to collect story material from sales, customer success, or directly from the customer.

---

## Story Metadata

- **Customer name:**
- **Industry / vertical:**
- **Customer size / scale:** (employees, sites, countries — whatever's relevant)
- **Primary persona:** (who lived the pain — CISO, Head of Ops, IT Director, etc.)
- **Secondary personas (the buying committee):** (who else had to approve — Finance? HR? IT?)
- **Deal type:** new logo / expansion / replacement
- **Specific product(s) / platform(s) used:** (avoid "the solution" — name them)

---

## The Outcome

- **Primary outcome / headline metric:** (€X saved, X% efficiency gain, X hours saved/week, zero incidents during [event])
- **Secondary outcomes:** (what else improved — visibility, compliance, headcount, satisfaction)

---

## The Before (Status Quo as Villain)

- What were they doing before? (spreadsheets, legacy system, manual process, nothing)
- What did that actually cost them? (time, money, risk, reputation — be specific)
- What was the breaking point — the moment doing nothing became riskier than changing?

---

## The Job-to-be-Done

Complete this sentence: *"When [situation], we needed to [job], so we could [desired outcome], without [constraint]."*

---

## The Buying Process

- What were the non-negotiables?
- Which departments had input, and what did each one need to sign off?
- Why did you choose this over the status quo or other options?

---

## Implementation

- What did Day 1 look like?
- What made the rollout easier than expected?
- When did the team first realise it was actually working?
