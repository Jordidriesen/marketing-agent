PASS if the post follows LinkedIn practice for a single post: the hook is specific and sits in the first 150 characters; paragraphs are short with blank lines between thoughts; the link to the landing page is not in the post body (it is placed in the first comment, with that said); there is no engagement bait such as "Agree? Comment below"; there are at most two hashtags and they are inline, not stacked at the end; no poll; and every product statement stays within the four facts in the brief, with no invented customers, numbers or results. FAIL otherwise.
