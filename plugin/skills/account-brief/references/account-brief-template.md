# Account brief template

One page per account. Every fact has a source and a date. Inference is labelled.

```markdown
# [Company name]: account brief

As of: [date] · Brand: [brand] · Prepared for: [sender]

## The company
- What they do: [one or two lines] ([source], [date])
- Legal entity and registry number: [..] ([registry], [date])
- Size band and locations: [..] ([source], [date])
- Relevant tech or suppliers, if public: [..] ([source], [date])

## Trigger events
| Date | Event | Why it matters for us | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [date] | [event] | [link to a pillar or product] | [url] |

No sourced trigger found: [say so, and what was checked]

## Buying committee (by role)
| Role | Function in the decision | Persona | Named person (public, professional source only) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [role] | decides / uses / signs / can block | [persona slug] | [name, source] or unknown |

## Fit
- Score: strong / partial / weak
- Why: [reasons against the ICP]
- What would change it: [..]

## Angle
[One sentence: sourced trigger plus the pillar or proof we can back.] (inference)

## Suggested first touch
- Channel and play: [email first touch / LinkedIn warm-up / connect with a note / ..]
- Why: [..]

## Open items
- [What could not be verified, what the user should confirm]
```
