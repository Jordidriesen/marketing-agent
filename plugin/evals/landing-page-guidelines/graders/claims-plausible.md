PASS if the copy stays within what the brief supports. Plausible claims that the brief does not confirm are allowed only when the copy is clean and a hidden HTML comment of the form confirm: ... sits next to them. Any claim the brief lists as not confirmed must carry such a hidden note. Statistics, customer names, logos, quotes, awards, certifications and prices must not be invented. The copy may promise what the offering is meant to fix, as the brief states it. FAIL if the copy invents proof, certifications or product mechanics the facts do not make very likely, or states an unconfirmed claim with no hidden note.
