# Research Sources and Cadence

Supporting reference for `competitive-brief`'s Research Process step.

## Primary sources (direct from competitor)

- **Website** — homepage, product pages, pricing, about, careers
- **Blog/resource center** — content themes, publishing frequency, depth
- **Social profiles** — messaging, engagement, content strategy
- **Product demos/free trials** — UX, features, onboarding
- **Webinars/events** — topics, speakers, audience engagement
- **Press releases/newsroom** — announcements, partnerships, milestones
- **Job postings** — hiring signals revealing strategic priorities (e.g. hiring for a new product line or market)

## Secondary sources (third-party)

- **Review sites** (G2, Capterra, TrustRadius, Product Hunt) — customer sentiment themes
- **Analyst reports** (Gartner, Forrester, IDC) — market positioning and category placement
- **News coverage** — funding, partnerships, narrative
- **Social listening** — mentions, sentiment, share of voice
- **SEO tools** — keyword rankings, organic traffic estimates, content gaps
- **Financial filings** — revenue, growth rate, investment areas (public companies only)
- **Community forums** (Reddit, Discourse, industry Slack communities) — unfiltered user sentiment

## Research cadence

- **Deep analysis** — quarterly, full research across all sources above
- **Monitoring** — monthly, scan for new announcements/content/messaging changes
- **Real-time alerts** — ongoing, for brand mentions, press, and job postings
