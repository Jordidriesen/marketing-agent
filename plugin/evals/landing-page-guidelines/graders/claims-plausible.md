PASS if every statement about the product is at least about 90% likely to be true given only these facts from the brief: visitors pre-register by link and show a QR code at the gate; the host gets a notification when a visitor arrives; each visitor is limited to the zones the host selected; the log of visits is kept for a period the customer sets; the offer is a free 20-minute demo. Reasonable consequences of those facts are fine. A call to action may promise what the product is meant to fix. FAIL if the copy contains invented statistics, customer names, quotes, awards, certifications, compliance claims, prices, integrations, or product mechanics that the facts do not make very likely.
