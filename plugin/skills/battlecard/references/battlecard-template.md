# Battlecard template

Fill every section from sourced facts. Delete a section only if it genuinely does not apply, and say
why in "Card control". Keep the card to one page. Everything else goes in the appendix.

```markdown
# Battlecard: [Our product] vs [Competitor]
Internal only. Owner: [name]. Reviewed: [date]. Next review: [date].

## Snapshot
- Who they are and who they sell to: [one line, sourced]
- How they position: [their own words, quoted, with source]
- Typical deal: [size, buyer, sales cycle, only if known]

## Where we win
| Difference | Why it matters to this buyer | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| [specific] | [consequence for the buyer] | [verified evidence, permission status] |

## Where they win
| Their strength | How to respond honestly |
|---|---|
| [specific] | [what we say, or "concede and move to fit"] |

## Parity (do not spend time here)
- [capability both have]

## Landmines: fair questions to ask the prospect
1. [Question that surfaces a real difference, answerable by the prospect themselves]
2. ...

## Traps: what they will say about us
| What they will say | Honest response |
|---|---|

## Objection responses
### "[Objection in the prospect's words]"
- Acknowledge: [what is fair in it]
- Reframe: [the question underneath]
- Proof: [evidence]

## Proof points
| Customer or result | Source | Permission to use |
|---|---|---|

## When not to compete
- [prospect profile where they are the better fit]

## Card control
- Sources: see appendix
- Weakest claims on this card: [list]
- Open items: [what to verify next]
```

## Appendix: source table

| Claim | Source | Date seen | Confidence (verified / reported / inference) |
|---|---|---|---|

## Appendix: to check

Inferences and unverified points. Not for use on a call until confirmed.
