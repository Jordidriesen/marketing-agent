## How to choose visitor management software for a warehouse

In today's fast-paced logistics landscape, choosing the right visitor management software is a pivotal decision that underscores the vital role of site security. Furthermore, a robust platform can serve as a testament to your commitment to safety.

Visitor management software records who is on site. Look at four things before you buy:

- **Pre-registration:** visitors register by link before the visit and show a QR code at the gate.
- **Host notification:** the host gets a message when the visitor arrives.
- **Zone limits:** each visitor can only enter the zones the host selected.
- **Retention:** the visit log is kept for a period you set.

It is not just about ticking boxes, it's about unlocking a seamless, holistic experience that fosters trust. Experts argue that a transformative approach to access control delves into the intricate interplay between people and places. Moreover, many businesses have already leveraged these cutting-edge tools to streamline their operations.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does visitor management software replace access control?**
No. It layers onto the access control hardware a site already has, so you add visitor management without starting over.

**How long is the visit log kept?**
For the period the customer sets.

**Can a visitor reach every zone?**
No. Each visitor is limited to the zones the host selected.
