# Metric Definitions and Benchmarks

Supporting reference for `performance-report`'s Key Metrics Dashboard section. For Google Ads-specific metrics and diagnostics, prefer `quality-score-doctor`, `device-performance-analyzer`, `geo-performance-analyzer`, and `metric-detective` over the generic Paid Advertising table below — they use live account data instead of industry benchmarks.

## Email Marketing

| Metric | Definition | Benchmark | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|---|
| Delivery rate | Delivered / sent | 95–99% | List health, sender reputation |
| Open rate | Unique opens / delivered | 15–30% | Subject line, sender effectiveness |
| CTR | Unique clicks / delivered | 2–5% | Content relevance, CTA effectiveness |
| Click-to-open rate | Unique clicks / unique opens | 10–20% | Content quality for those who opened |
| Unsubscribe rate | Unsubscribes / delivered | <0.5% | Content-audience fit, frequency tolerance |
| Bounce rate | Bounces / sent | <2% | List quality, data hygiene |
| Conversion rate | Conversions / delivered | 1–5% | End-to-end email effectiveness |
| List growth rate | (New subs − unsubs) / total list | 2–5% monthly | Audience-building health |

## Social Media

| Metric | Definition | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Impressions | Times content was displayed | Distribution and reach |
| Reach | Unique users who saw content | Audience breadth |
| Engagement rate | (Likes+comments+shares) / reach | Content resonance |
| CTR | Link clicks / impressions | Traffic-driving effectiveness |
| Follower growth rate | Net new followers / total per period | Audience building |
| Video view/completion rate | Views or completions / impressions or views | Hook and length fit |
| Social share of voice | Your mentions / total category mentions | Brand visibility vs. competitors |

## Paid Advertising (Search and Social)

| Metric | Definition | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| CTR | Clicks / impressions | Ad creative, targeting relevance |
| CPC | Spend / clicks | Cost efficiency of traffic |
| CPM | Cost per 1,000 impressions | Awareness cost efficiency |
| Conversion rate | Conversions / clicks | Landing page, offer effectiveness |
| CPA | Spend / conversions | Full-funnel cost efficiency |
| ROAS | Revenue / spend | Revenue generation efficiency |
| Quality Score (search) | Google's 1–10 relevance rating | Ad-keyword-landing page alignment |
| Frequency | Avg. times a user sees the ad | Ad fatigue risk |

## SEO / Organic Search

| Metric | Definition | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Organic sessions | Visits from organic search | SEO reach |
| Keyword rankings | Position for target terms | Search visibility |
| Organic CTR | Clicks / impressions in results | Title/meta effectiveness |
| Pages indexed | Pages in the search index | Crawlability, site health |
| Backlinks | External sites linking in | Content authority |
| Organic conversion rate | Organic conversions / organic sessions | Content-intent alignment |

## Content Marketing

| Metric | Definition | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| Pageviews / unique visitors | Reach of content pages | Distribution, audience size |
| Avg. time on page | Engagement depth | Content quality |
| Bounce rate | Single-page sessions / total | Content-audience fit |
| Social shares | Shares of the content | Resonance, virality |
| Content ROI | Revenue attributed / production cost | Investment return |

## Overall Marketing / Pipeline

| Metric | Definition | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| MQLs / SQLs | Qualified leads at each stage | Funnel effectiveness, lead quality |
| MQL→SQL conversion rate | SQLs / MQLs | Marketing-sales alignment |
| Pipeline generated | $ value of opportunities created | Marketing's revenue impact |
| CAC | (Marketing+sales cost) / new customers | Acquisition efficiency |
| CAC payback period | Months to recover CAC | Unit economics health |
| Marketing-sourced / -influenced revenue | Revenue from marketing-originated / marketing-touched deals | Direct vs. broader contribution |
