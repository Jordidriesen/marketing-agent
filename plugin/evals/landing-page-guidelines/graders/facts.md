PASS if every product claim in the copy traces to these four facts from the brief: visitors pre-register by link and show a QR code at the gate; the host gets a notification when a visitor arrives; each visitor is limited to the zones the host selected; the log of visits is kept for a period the customer sets. The only offer is a free 20-minute demo. Audience details from the request (mid-sized warehouses, facility managers, Google Ads traffic) are allowed. There must be no invented statistics, customers, compliance claims, integrations, service promises or demo contents, and missing proof must appear as a placeholder inside the copy. Consequences that follow directly from a stated fact (for example, the host is told when a visitor arrives) are allowed; a general description of the audience's situation in the Problem or Agitate step of PAS or StoryBrand is allowed when it makes no product claim; invented incidents, numbers or scenarios that assert something about the product or its effects are not. FAIL otherwise.
