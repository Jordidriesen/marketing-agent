PASS if all hold. (1) The page carries a hidden HTML comment implementation brief that covers the URL, the ad group, the framework, ONE conversion goal ("Book a 20-minute demo"), the form fields, and a meta title and description. (2) Every section is tagged with one of the allowed components (hero with logo strip, sticky anchor nav, three problem cards, capability tabs, trust cards, reference cards, FAQ accordion, closing CTA panel), either listed in the brief or in a comment above the section. The contact form modal is not a page component and does not count against this. (3) Every primary call to action names the same action, and any secondary path is described as smaller or lower contrast than the primary one. (4) There is an FAQ of 4 to 6 questions that answer objections this traffic arrives with. (5) Proof appears only as bracketed placeholders (marking them 'add once approved' is fine) and no heading or line implies customers that do not exist, such as 'trusted by' or 'operators like yours' without a placeholder. FAIL otherwise.
